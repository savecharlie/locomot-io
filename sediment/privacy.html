SEDIMENT — Privacy Policy

Effective: March 14, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Sediment does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Local storage

The game saves your progress (current level, death count, settings) to your device using localStorage. This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time through your browser or app settings.

Network requests

Sediment loads its game files from locomot.io on first launch. After that, the service worker caches everything for offline play. No analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts.

Accounts

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no registration.

Advertising

There are no ads.

In-app purchases

There are no in-app purchases.

Children

Sediment does not knowingly collect any information from anyone. The game is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here.
